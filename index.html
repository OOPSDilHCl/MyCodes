Our story

Two friends, one grill, and too much lemon.

Mario ran the kitchen his grandmother taught him in Corfu; Adrian handled the front of house and the wine list. In 1971 they pooled their savings for a single charcoal grill and a crate of lemons from the market next door — the crate never really emptied. Every dish since has kept that same instinct: char something over wood, finish it with citrus, serve it to people like family.
